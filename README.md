# south-asia-climate-crops
# Climate and Crop Yields in South Asia

## Question
How much of the year to year variation in wheat and rice yields across
South Asian countries is explained by temperature and rainfall, and can a
model predict yields better than a simple time trend?

## Scope
- Region: Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Afghanistan, Bhutan
- Crops: wheat and rice
- Period: 1961 to latest available year

## Hypotheses
1. Higher growing season temperatures reduce wheat yield.
2. Rice yield depends more on monsoon rainfall than wheat does.

## Data sources (to be confirmed)
- FAOSTAT: crop yields
- World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal: temperature and rainfall
